\# HueHeritage - AI



\### AI-Powered Inclusive Makeup Intelligence



\## 📌 Project Overview



HueHeritage AI is an AI-powered beauty assistant designed to make makeup recommendations more inclusive, personalized, and accessible across diverse skin tones and undertones.



Traditional makeup recommendation systems often struggle with the natural diversity of human skin, particularly across deeper and underrepresented skin tones. HueHeritage AI aims to address this gap by analyzing a user's skin characteristics and providing personalized makeup shade recommendations based on their individual complexion and undertone rather than relying on generic shade categories.



\## 🎯 Problem Statement



Finding the right makeup shade can be challenging because many existing beauty tools and shade-matching systems are not equally optimized for diverse skin tones and undertones.



Users may experience:



\* Limited or inaccurate shade recommendations

\* Difficulty identifying their undertone

\* Recommendations that do not account for variations in lighting and complexion

\* Lack of personalized guidance when selecting makeup products



This creates a gap between the diversity of real users and the personalization offered by existing beauty technology.



\## 💡 Proposed Solution



HueHeritage AI proposes an intelligent makeup recommendation system that combines computer vision and AI-based analysis to understand a user's skin characteristics and generate personalized recommendations.



The system will aim to:



1\. Analyze an uploaded facial image.

2\. Identify relevant skin-tone characteristics.

3\. Estimate the user's undertone.

4\. Generate suitable makeup shade recommendations.

5\. Explain why a particular shade is recommended.

6\. Provide alternative recommendations where appropriate.



\## ✨ Key Features



\* 🧑‍🎨 \*\*Skin Tone Analysis\*\* — Analyze visible skin characteristics from an image.

\* 🎨 \*\*Undertone Detection\*\* — Estimate warm, cool, or neutral undertones.

\* 💄 \*\*Personalized Shade Matching\*\* — Recommend suitable foundation, concealer, blush, and lip shades.

\* 🧠 \*\*AI-Based Recommendations\*\* — Generate recommendations based on analyzed characteristics.

\* 🔍 \*\*Explainable Recommendations\*\* — Explain the reasoning behind suggested shades.

\* 🌍 \*\*Inclusive Design\*\* — Designed with diverse skin tones as a core consideration.

\* 📊 \*\*Confidence-Aware Results\*\* — Communicate uncertainty when image conditions may affect analysis.

\## 🧠 AI / ML Approach



The project will explore computer vision and machine-learning techniques for skin analysis and recommendation generation.



The planned pipeline includes:



\* Image preprocessing

\* Face and skin-region detection

\* Skin-color feature extraction

\* Skin-tone classification

\* Undertone estimation

\* Recommendation generation



The final model and implementation approach will be selected based on accuracy, computational requirements, available datasets, and fairness across diverse skin tones.
